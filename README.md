# DevTrace

**The workspace for GGSIPU engineering students chasing a placement.**

Everything you juggle during placement season — DSA practice, interview prep, notes, focus time, your GitHub activity — in one place, instead of nine browser tabs and a WhatsApp group.

**[devtracedash.netlify.app →](https://devtracedash.netlify.app)**

---

## The problem

Placement season at GGSIPU runs on rumours. Which companies are visiting this month? What did Nagarro actually ask in Round 2? Is there a bond? What CGPA do they filter at? The answers live in seniors' heads and expiring WhatsApp messages — and by the time you find them, the drive has moved on.

Meanwhile you're tracking DSA progress in a spreadsheet, notes in another app, and your GitHub streak nowhere at all.

DevTrace puts the answers and the tracking in the same place.

---

## What's inside

### Placement Prep Hub

The part you can't get anywhere else — real interview intelligence from IPU campuses, not generic prep content.

- **Company directory** — companies that visit IPU campuses, with round structures, eligibility rules, service bonds, and actual selection rates
- **Interview debriefs** — round-by-round accounts from placed seniors: what was asked, what to prepare, what tripped people up
- **Question bank** — past questions filterable by company, subject, round, and difficulty
- **Topic trends** — which subjects each company leans on, weighted by how often they appear in real rounds, so you prepare for what's actually tested
- **Campus calendar** — which companies run drives in which month, before the season starts
- **Prep roadmaps** — a per-company checklist ordered by what matters most, plus the HR questions that keep coming up

Currently covering **16 companies**, **22 interview debriefs** and **50 tagged questions**.

### DSA Tracker

A **79-problem curriculum across 23 days**, grouped by topic from arrays through dynamic programming. Tick problems off and your progress follows you across devices. Today's Focus surfaces the next unsolved problem, so you never open the app wondering where you left off.

### Developer Card

One shareable card combining your DSA progress, streak, campus and GitHub stats. Export it as an image for class groups or LinkedIn — no manual screenshots.

### Your workspace

- **Tasks, notes and goals** — one view, with priorities, due dates and pinning
- **Pomodoro timer** — 25/5 focus cycles with a nudge when each block ends
- **Code snippets** — the utilities you keep rewriting, saved by language, one click to copy
- **API sandbox** — send any HTTP request and read the response without leaving the app or installing anything
- **GitHub profile** — connect once for your contribution heatmap, streaks, top repositories and open pull requests

### Made yours

Four themes — Trace, Neon, Black and White — switchable from anywhere. Fully responsive, so it works on the phone you actually check between lectures.
